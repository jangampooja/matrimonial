About ShaadiMatch

Where meaningful stories begin.

ShaadiMatch is a front-end demonstration of a matrimonial matchmaking platform. It brings profile creation, discovery, preferences and mutual-interest features together in one welcoming experience. The sample profiles and interactions are demonstration data, not verified real members.

Our goal is to make the journey of discovering a compatible partner clear, respectful and easy to use.
